# V3-9 · Selects: a modern select and combobox across the app

**Agent:** (unclaimed) · **Branch:** `v3/V3-9-selects` (base `design-v3`) · **Port:** 5239
**Approved:** yes (26 Nov 2026): **Bits UI**, native picker on phones

## Why
You asked for a more modern select (26 Nov).
- Today there are **125 native `<select>`s in 45 web files**. V3-0 gave them a matching chevron, but the open list is still the browser's own.
- Long lists (suppliers, products, warehouses, trucks) also need typing to search.

## Options (checked 26 Nov 2026)
| Option | Fit |
|---|---|
| **Bits UI 2.x** (`bits-ui`, MIT, Svelte 5: Select, Combobox, Popover) | **Recommended.** Headless and accessible (keyboard, screen readers, phone), styled with our tokens; the same pieces give us the menu, popover and date picker later. |
| Our own component (like the new company select in V3-1b) | Small, but we would have to write and maintain all the accessibility ourselves. |
| svelte-select | Ready-made, but it has its own look and is less maintained for Svelte 5. |

## Proposed approach
1. Add two components to `@btw/ui`:
   - `Select`: a short list with an optional mark/icon per option, a tick on the chosen one, and a clear button;
   - `Combobox`: type to search, groups, "No match".
   Same height and focus ring as the inputs; light + dark.
2. Replace the native selects area by area: filters first (list pages), then forms (Create PO, period plan, master data), then dashboard filters.
3. Keep a native `<select>` on phone-size screens where the phone's own picker is better (the field app), unless you prefer the new look there too.

## Decision needed from you
1. **Library:** Bits UI (recommended) or our own?
2. **Phones:** native picker on phones (recommended), or the new look everywhere?
