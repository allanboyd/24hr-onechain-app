## Goal
Everyone can open their own profile and manage their account without asking an admin: see who they are and what they can see, fix their name and email, change their own password or PIN, and set their preferences.

## What exists today (inspected 26 Nov)
- **Top bar:** the name chip shows the name and role but does nothing.
- **Password / PIN:** only an admin sets them (`POST /users/:id/secret`, Users & access). That ends every session of the user.
- **Own details:** no way to change your own name or email.
- **My notifications:** email / SMS for money news, in Settings & alerts.
- **Theme:** light / dark switch in the top bar.
- **Devices:** phones signed in to the field app are recorded (`devices`), but no screen shows them to their owner.

## Proposal
**Where:** inside **Settings & alerts**, a first group **My account** with:
- **Profile** (new)
- **My notifications** (moved from the Alerts group)

The name chip in the top bar opens Profile. Everyone has this group, whatever their role.

**Profile, in three compact blocks:**
1. **Details:**
   - name and email, editable, with Save / Cancel;
   - phone, read-only: it is the field-app sign-in, so the admin changes it;
   - roles, companies, warehouses, loading points and supplier pairs, read-only: "what you can see". Changes are requested from the admin.
2. **Sign-in and security:**
   - **Change password** for web sign-in: current password, new password twice, at least 8 characters;
   - **Change PIN** for field-app users: current PIN, new PIN, 4 to 6 digits;
   - both ask for the current one; every change goes to the audit log;
   - **Signed-in phones:** the field-app devices on your account (platform, app version, last seen), with **Sign out this phone**, after a confirm step.
3. **Preferences:**
   - theme: light, dark or follow the device;
   - the company to open in (Group or one company, for people with more than one).
   - Kept on this device, like the theme today.

**Server:**
- `PATCH /me` (name, email);
- `POST /me/secret` (current + new password or PIN);
- `GET /me/devices`, `DELETE /me/devices/:id`.
- The same in the browser demo (mock). Audit entries for each.

## Decisions for you
1. **After changing your own password or PIN:**
   - (a) your other sessions end and you stay signed in here (**proposed**);
   - (b) you are signed out everywhere and sign in again.
2. **Name and email:**
   - (a) each person can change their own (**proposed**, audit logged);
   - (b) only the admin can.
3. **Profile photo:**
   - (a) not now, initials stay (**proposed**: nothing stores a photo today);
   - (b) add a photo upload (uses the existing file store).
