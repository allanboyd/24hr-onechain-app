# V3-12 · Money records that come from the work itself

## Goal
The figures that Management shows as "Not recorded yet" must be filled by the steps people already do in Operations and on the phone app, not by forms that exist only for the reports.

Rule for this row: **every new table is written by a process step**. A table is written either:
- by a step that already exists (selling, loading, approving), or
- by a small step added where the event really happens (a client pays, a supplier sends a pro-forma, cash is banked).

Management only reads these tables. It never gets its own data entry.

Replaces V3-10 step 4.

## What is missing today, and where it really happens

| Missing figure | Where it happens in our process | Today |
|---|---|---|
| Client credit terms | The client, on the **sale** | Client is free text on the sale (`sales.customer`), with a fixed list of four names on the web form. Every client gets the same 14 days. |
| Client payments by date | **Selling** (paid at once) and later **collecting** | `sales.paid` is set once when the sale is made. A later payment can't be recorded, so Cash "received" is approximated. |
| Pro-forma invoices | **Purchase order**: the supplier answers the PO with a pro-forma | Not recorded |
| Advance payments to suppliers | **Purchase order**: paid against the pro-forma before loading | Not recorded |
| Short loads (money left with the supplier) | **Loading**: the loader records loaded quantities (PO revised at loading) | Already derived from the loadings: no new entry |
| Credit notes (promotions) | **Supplier**: the supplier sends a credit note | Not recorded |
| Using money held by a supplier | **Next advance** to the same supplier | Not recorded |
| Opening cash | **Setup** of each warehouse's cash, once | Not recorded |
| Transfers to the company account | **Warehouse cash**: the cashier banks the takings | Not recorded |

## Design

### A. Clients and credit terms (Sales process)
- **New table `clients`:**
  - columns: id, entity, name, warehouse (optional), phone, credit days (default 14, from `TERMS_DAYS`), active;
  - unique on (entity, name).
- **Migration:** creates one client for each distinct `sales.customer` and adds `sales.client_id`. The name on the sale stays, as history.
- **Sale form (web `Sales.svelte` and phone `NewSale.svelte`):**
  - the client is picked from the list;
  - typing a new name creates the client **in the same save**, with 14 days;
  - "Walk-in" stays as the default cash client.
- **Master data → Clients tab:** name, phone, credit days, active, with what each client owes and how much is overdue.
- **Rule `overdueAfter(client)`** in `rules.ts`: the client's own credit days, or 14 days if not set. Used by `MX.overdueByCustomers`, so Sales, Live dashboards and Management agree.

### B. Client payments (Selling → Collecting)
- **New table `client_payments`:** id, entity, sale, client, amount, currency, date, method (cash / mobile money / bank), reference, recorded by.
- **Making a sale** with an amount paid writes the first payment automatically. A cash sale is paid at once; nothing new to type.
- **New step "Record payment"** on an unpaid sale, on the web Sales page and the phone Sales screen:
  - amount, method, reference;
  - it can't be more than the balance.
- `sales.paid` and `sales.status` are updated **in the same transaction**, so every existing screen keeps working.
- **Migration:** one payment per existing sale with `paid > 0`, dated at the sale.
- **Management:**
  - Cash "received" becomes the payments dated in the period, not "paid on the period's sales";
  - Sales and clients shows "How clients pay" by method.

### C. Supplier money (Purchase order → Loading → next order)
- **New table `supplier_entries`:** id, entity, supplier, PO (optional), kind, amount, currency, rate, USD, date, reference, document, note, recorded by, status, approved by.
  - Kinds: `proforma`, `advance`, `credit-note`, `use`.
  - Short loads are **not** stored. They stay derived from the loadings, as Supplier balances does today, so there is one source.
- **PO page (`POView.svelte`), after the PO is sent:**
  - **"Add pro-forma"**: the supplier's amount, reference and file;
  - **"Record advance payment"**: paid against the pro-forma. It can't be more than the open pro-forma. It records who entered it and when.
    - Approval is set in **Management → Settings**: "Advance payments need director approval", **off by default** (your decision, 26 Nov).
    - Off: the advance counts at once.
    - On: it waits as `Pending approval` until a director approves, like transport payments.
- **Loading:** no change. The short load already revises the PO; the supplier balance updates by itself.
- **Credit note:** recorded on the supplier's statement (Master data → Suppliers → the supplier), with the document, or on the PO it relates to.
- **Using held money:** when recording the next advance to the same supplier, the form shows "Money with this supplier: $X (short loads + credit notes)" and lets you use part of it. That writes a `use` entry, which lowers the balance.
- **Management → Supplier balances:**
  - the statement becomes: pro-formas, advances, short loads, credit notes, uses, then the balance;
  - the "not recorded" flags go.

### D. Warehouse cash: opening cash and transfers
- **New table `cash_entries`:** id, warehouse, receiving company, kind (`opening` / `transfer`), amount, currency, rate, USD, date, reference, slip file, note, recorded by.
- **Opening cash, at both levels (your decision, 26 Nov):**
  - **Receiving company:** cash at start, with its date and currency, set on the Warehouse expenses page (company view). This is the **default for its warehouses**: they take its date and currency.
  - **Warehouse:** can set its own amount. Whatever part of the company amount isn't given to a warehouse shows as "Company, not split by warehouse".
  - Warehouse amounts can't add up to more than the company amount.
  - Only the director or finance can change it later, and each change is in the audit log.
- **New step "Send to the company account"** on the Warehouse expenses page (the warehouse cashier banks the takings): amount, date, bank slip. It sits next to the expenses, because the same person records both.
- **Management → Cash and closing:**
  - cash in hand = opening + received − bills − transfers;
  - the "not recorded" flags go.

### E. Settings and notifications (your decision, 26 Nov)
- **New page: Management → Settings** (directors and the system administrator):
  - **Approvals:** "Advance payments to suppliers need director approval" (off by default). It is stored in `settings`, like the payment approval threshold.
  - **Who is told:** money events (advance recorded, credit note recorded, cash sent to the company) go to the directors.
- **Every money record** saves who recorded it and when, and sends an **in-app notification to the directors** (`notify.service`).
- **Each director chooses their channels** under Settings → My notifications: in-app always, **email** and **SMS** optional (off by default).
  - This adds `notify_email` / `notify_sms` to `users`; the SMS uses the phone in Users and access.
  - Delivery uses the existing mail and SMS ports.

### Shared rules
- USD at the approved month-average rate (`fxFor`), as warehouse expenses do.
- Every write goes to the audit log and is idempotent, the same as sales and expenses.
- Access:
  - clients and payments: anyone with the `sales` feature;
  - pro-formas, advances and credit notes: the PO roles, and the director approves;
  - cash: warehouse money roles.
- The mock (`packages/api/src/mock`) gets the same records and demo data, so the demo shows filled figures.
