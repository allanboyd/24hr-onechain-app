# V3-15 · Shipments with the map, Truck desk, Alerts as activity, Stock made easy

**Agent:** claude · **Branch:** `v3/V3-15-live-pages` · **Worktree:** `../btw-gd-V3-15` · **Port:** 5246
**Approved:** yes (26 Nov 2026): 1 Truck desk in the Settings & alerts left-menu layout, unless research finds a better design; 2 Shipments open in split view; 3 Board as cards grouped by state

## Goal
Make the pages people watch all day easy to follow at a glance, and clean, with nothing new to learn:
- shipments and their map on one page;
- the Truck desk in a clean workspace;
- alerts as an activity list with buttons;
- a Stock page that doesn't make people scroll sideways.

## What I saw (26 Nov, shared app)
- **Shipments** and **Shipment map** are two sidebar entries for one page:
  - the list is a wide table (company, shipment, truck, route, last check-in, next checkpoint, status, ETA) with three to four lines per row;
  - the map view lists trucks on the left with red labels piled on top of each other;
  - picking a truck does not focus the map;
  - nothing shows a shipment's journey (where it has been, where it goes next).
- **Truck desk:** four tabs (Board, Queues, Providers & trucks, Costs) under a big header; the board is a table.
- **Alerts:**
  - the Inbox is a table of notifications;
  - nothing groups them by day or shows what each one is about at a glance;
  - each has only a row click.
- **Stock:**
  - an 11-column table (received, damaged, sent on, sold, on hand, in transit, level…) that runs off the right edge at 1440 px;
  - the figures are right, but the page doesn't say what to do.

## Proposal

### 1. Shipments: one page, list and map together
- One sidebar entry **Shipments**; the **Shipment map** entry goes. Old `?view=map` links still open the map.
- **Wide screens: split view.**
  - Left, a list of **shipment cards**: truck and shipment number, route `Nairobi → Gisenyi`, a **progress bar** of checkpoints passed (loading point → borders → warehouse), state badge, ETA, last check-in "2 h ago via SMS", and a red line when something is wrong (late, no check-in).
  - Right, the map. Hovering or picking a card **flies the map** to that truck and draws its route (done part solid, rest dashed). Labels don't overlap: they group when zoomed out.
- **Picking a shipment** opens a side panel with:
  - the **journey timeline**: each checkpoint with planned and actual time, the current one highlighted, the next one with its ETA;
  - the truck, driver (call / SMS) and cases;
  - an **Open shipment** button.
- **View switch:** List | Split | Map, kept in the address. The current table stays as the "List" view, for export and long searches.
- The status tiles stay on top, as the filters.
- **Phones:** cards first, with a Map button that opens the map full screen.

### 2. Truck desk: the clean workspace layout
- The same left-menu layout as Settings & alerts, cleaner:
  - **Board, Queues, Providers & trucks, Costs** in a narrow menu on the left, with counts (e.g. "Queues 3 waiting");
  - the chosen part on the right, with its own address.
- **Board:** truck cards grouped by state (Waiting to load, Loading, On the road, At a border, Delayed, Arrived), each with the same progress bar and a one-line "what next". Picking a truck opens the existing truck drawer. A table view stays available.
- **Queues:** one column per loading point with numbered truck cards you can drag, wait time, and a big **Record loading** button.
- **Costs:** the tables I just moved stay; a short summary row on top (owed, waiting approval, not recorded).

- **Research (26 Nov):** dispatch tools keep the **board and the live map on one screen**, use **a small, consistent set of states**, and **flag problems on the truck itself** (papers, driver). So the Board gets state columns **and a map panel side by side**, and each card shows flags: no driver, papers missing, no check-in, long wait. Sources: datatruck.io dispatch board, onfleet.com dashboard map refresh, trackroad.com live dispatching.

### 3. Alerts: an activity list
- **Inbox becomes an activity feed**, grouped **Today / Yesterday / This week / Older**:
  - each item has an icon for its area (truck, order, warehouse, money, sales);
  - a coloured dot when unread, the title in plain words, one line of detail, and the time;
  - buttons: **Open**, **Mark read** (or **Mark unread**).
  - On top: filter chips by area (All, Fleet, Purchasing, Warehouse, Money, Sales), Unread only, and **Mark all read**.
- **Open exceptions become cards grouped by severity** (Critical, High, Medium, Low):
  - each with what happened, who acts, how long ago, and an **Open** button;
  - type and area chips to narrow them.
- The table stays reachable via a "Table" switch, for export.

### 4. Stock: easy to read
- **Top:** one card per warehouse: stock value, cases on hand, in transit, and how many products are low, out or overstocked. Picking a card filters the page.
- **List:** one row per product, short:
  - brand and pack, warehouse;
  - **on hand** with a **cover bar** (days of stock, coloured by level: out, low, OK, overstock);
  - in transit, level badge, and a short "what to do" (order more, move to another warehouse, stop ordering).
- **Details:** received, damaged, sent on and sold move into a panel that opens on the row. Nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Group by:** a switch between Warehouse and Product (all warehouses of one product side by side).
- **Count / adjust stock** stays where it is.

  - an **activity item**;
  - a **cover bar**.
