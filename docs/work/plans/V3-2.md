# V3-2 · Management room: the directors' report as is

## Goal
Directors open Management and see the report from `docs/design-v3/reference/management-room-report.html`, **unchanged**, inside our app. Our sidebar picks the page and a banner says it is sample data. The live dashboards (Home) are also listed under Management (the 26 Nov decision: Home is duplicated for now).

## Files I will touch
- `apps/web/public/management/report.html`: a byte-for-byte copy of the reference file (checked with `cmp`)
- `apps/web/src/screens/management/Management.svelte` (new): a same-origin frame. On load it:
  - hides the report's own sidebar (`aside.side`) and collapses its 210 px column,
  - opens the page from the route through the report's own `show()` (keys whitelisted: `overview exec plans clients suppliers cash perf fleet stock expenses`),
  - shows the sample-data banner, with a link to Live dashboards.
- `apps/web/src/routes.ts` (**Request to V3-1**, which owns it):
  - the routes `/management` and `/management/:page`,
  - a temporary **Management** nav group (Overview, Executive report, Plans and orders, Sales and clients, Supplier balances, Cash and closing, Warehouse performance, Live dashboards → `#/`), gated by the `reports` feature.
  V3-1 moves the group under the Operations | Management switch.
