# V3-6 · Phone look: bottom bar, modern top bar (web on phones and the field app), login

**Agent:** claude · **Branch:** `v3/V3-6-phone` · **Worktree:** `../btw-gd-V3-6` · **Port:** 5236 (web) / 5226 (field)
**Approved:** yes (26 Nov 2026): bottom bar places per role; theme and sign-out move to Profile and More, and the freed top-bar space gets a **search** (pages, shipments, trucks, orders) and other quick actions
**After:** V3-15 (your order, 26 Nov)

## Goal
On a phone the office app should feel like an app, not a shrunk website:
- a bottom bar for the few places people go most;
- a slim modern top bar;
- the field app with the same look.

## What I saw (390 px, 26 Nov)
- **Web on a phone:**
  - the top bar is a big rounded card (menu button, page name, theme, bell, avatar, sign out) that takes about 90 px;
  - the menu is a slide-in drawer;
  - there is no bottom bar;
  - on Home, the filters take a whole screen before any figure.
- **Field app:** has its own tab bar for the team; its top bar is plain.

## Proposal
### Web app at phone width (≤ 760 px)
- **Bottom bar** with 5 places, chosen by role:
  - Office (director, planner, operations): **Home, Shipments, Trucks, Alerts, More**.
  - Warehouse (receiver): **Home, Stock, Sales, Alerts, More**.
  - Finance (accounts): **Home, Sales, Costs, Alerts, More**.
  - **More** opens the full menu as a sheet from the bottom, grouped like the sidebar, with the company switch and Operations | Management.
  - Alerts shows the unread count.
  - It hides while typing (keyboard open) and respects the phone's safe area.
- **Top bar, slim (56 px):**
  - the company mark and page name on the left;
  - bell and avatar on the right; the avatar opens Profile;
  - theme and sign-out move into Profile / More; the freed space gets a **search** (jump to a page, shipment, truck, PO or client) (your 26 Nov note);
  - it becomes solid with a soft shadow once you scroll.
- **Page headers** get smaller on phones. Filter bars collapse into a **Filters** button with a count, opening a bottom sheet. Home's filters go here.
- Tables already turn into cards on phones (V3-8); the cards get the bottom bar's spacing.

### Field app
- The same slim top bar: company mark, screen name, sync state (online / offline / pending), avatar.
- Its existing bottom tab bar restyled to match: icons with labels, active pill, badge counts.
- Its sheets match the web's bottom sheets.

### Login (web and phone, folds in UI-2)
- One clean card: company marks, "Sign in", phone + PIN for the field app or username + password for the web, a show / hide eye, clear errors.
- The demo sign-in list stays below in demo mode only.

## Decisions for you
1. **Bottom bar places per role:** as above (proposed), or one set for everyone (Home, Shipments, Trucks, Alerts, More).
2. **Theme and sign-out on phones:** in Profile and More (proposed), or keep them in the top bar.
