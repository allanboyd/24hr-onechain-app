## Goal
The sidebar and top bar look and feel like a modern app:
- the company logo at the top of the sidebar, with a clean company select under it,
- menu items without group titles (same order),
- a switch that slides,
- a styled top bar with the page title,
- a sidebar you can open and close at will (remembered).

## Issues (your 26 Nov list)

| SB-1 | Logo at the top when a company is selected | One brand header of fixed height. The company logo sits on a white tile, sized so all three (BTW, NUD, Kivu) are readable. Group = the three marks + "Group". No coloured underline. |
| SB-2 | Remove the menu group titles | Group labels go; a small gap between groups keeps the order readable. The labels stay in the code as `aria-label`s for screen readers. |

| SB-5 | Modern top bar | 60 px; light translucent background with blur; a hairline that turns into a soft shadow when the page scrolls. Left: the sidebar toggle and the **page title** (S-1). Right: theme, bell (pill badge), account chip (avatar initial, name, role) and sign out. |
| SB-6 | Open and close the sidebar at will | A toggle in the top bar. Desktop: full width (248 px) ↔ icon rail (72 px, tooltips, the logo becomes the mark), animated, remembered on this device. Phone: the existing drawer. Keyboard: Ctrl/⌘ + B. |
| - | Nav polish | Hover and active transitions; the active item gets a soft fill + the accent bar. |
