## Goal
The Live dashboards (Home tabs) read like the Management report: a calm grid of cards, KPIs first, paired charts, nothing stretched. On **Fleet**, the whole board fits the screen: each section has a maximum height and scrolls inside itself, so the page does not need to scroll.

| LD-2 | Arrange content like the reports | Per tab: KPI row first (the report's KPI card: label, value, change chip, spark), then paired cards (two columns), lists on the right, wide charts full width. | LD-3 | Same look as Management | Card titles, chips ("x items", "Total …"), the same filter bar (warehouse chips + period). Method text stays on the info button, not under the chart. |
| LD-4 | Keep what works | Data, builders, links and the `.cursor/rules/dashboards-home.mdc` rules stay; UI-5's header fix (D-1…D-4) is folded in here. |
